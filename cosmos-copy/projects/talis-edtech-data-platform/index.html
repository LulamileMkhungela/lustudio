TALIS. EdTech data platform design & development

About

TALIS is an international study by the OECD on the teaching and learning environment and the working conditions of teachers. We designed and developed a digital platform that explains the goals of the education research, presents its results and turns complex data into accessible insights.

Challenge

TALIS is an international OECD survey of teaching and learning environments and teachers' working conditions. The platform had to explain the goals of the survey, present its results and turn large volumes of analytical data into simple, visual insights. Special attention went to intuitive navigation, bilingual access, performance on slow connections and trust in the research among a broad audience.

No items found.
No items found.

Solution

We designed an intuitive, bilingual EdTech platform with low-bandwidth support and clear data visualization. A thoughtful content structure makes the survey results accessible to teachers and a wide audience. The website amplifies teachers' voices and helps people better understand the challenges of modern education.

No items found.

Development

Our team developed the bilingual platform in-house, with support for low-bandwidth connections and clear data visualizations. The result is a smooth, user-friendly experience that strengthens trust in OECD education research.
