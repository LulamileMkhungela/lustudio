Our approach

How we design wellness products people return to

Wellness app retention starts long before the first screen: with knowing why people would come back. Cosmos Studio’s approach depends on where your product is: a new app or brand starting from zero, or a website or app that already has users.

01

Starting from zero or improving what’s live

A new product starts with a discovery talk and hypotheses. For a live website or app, Cosmos Studio runs a UX audit: business metrics, real usage data, the problems, concepts to solve them, and A/B testing.

02

Hypotheses from real customers

If you haven’t interviewed your customers yet, Cosmos Studio runs interviews with The Mom Test method or gives your team the questions. Competitor analysis shows the gaps, like the social side of fasting that other trackers ignored.

03

Roles, flows, and the customer journey map

Cosmos Studio defines user roles, onboarding, tracking, and subscription flows, and a customer journey map, with AI helping us process the research.

04

Interactive prototypes in code

The moodboard grows together with the research. Cosmos Studio then shows several design concepts and builds the chosen one as a working, interactive prototype (UI engineering).

05

Health content people can read

Cosmos Studio designs follow Nielsen Norman Group usability heuristics, WCAG accessibility guidelines, and legibility standards. For Zeuss, we also art-directed 100+ photos of real people instead of clinical stock.

06

Designed, built, and measured by one team

The same designers build the front end, the design system in code, and the motion. Senior backend specialists handle security and architecture, and analytics after launch show what to improve.

How a project runs

01Cosmos Studio

Discovery talk

Cosmos Studio learns about your users, business model, key metrics, and launch date.

02Cosmos Studio

Customer research

If you already know your customers well, we go straight to hypotheses. If not, customer interviews with The Mom Test method, run by Cosmos Studio or by your team with our questions, plus competitor analysis.

03Cosmos Studio

Roles, flows, and customer journey map

Onboarding, tracking, subscription, and checkout flows, with AI helping us connect the research data.

04Cosmos Studio

Moodboard and design concepts

The moodboard grows together with the research. Then several design concepts, built on your brand if you have one, or a new brand identity if you need one.

05Cosmos Studio

Interactive prototype

The chosen concept becomes a working, coded prototype of the key flows (UI engineering).

06Cosmos Studio

Design and development

The final design follows Nielsen Norman Group heuristics, WCAG, and legibility standards. The same designers build the front end in React, Next.js, and TypeScript or on WordPress and Webflow, with the design system in code and all motion.

07Cosmos Studio

Launch and analytics

Launch, then analytics such as Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to watch the metrics and improve, as Cosmos Studio still does with new pages for Zeuss.

Starting from zero vs improving a live product

01Cosmos Studio

Starting from zero

02Cosmos Studio

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

1

What health and wellness products do you design?

Cosmos Studio designs wellness e-commerce and subscription websites, fitness and habit tracker apps, and sleep and wellness brands.

Our wellness work includes the Zeuss e-commerce website, the Fastmates fasting app, and the Remroom brand and website.

2

How much does wellness website or app design cost?

Projects start at $5,000, with hourly rates from $50.

The main cost drivers are the number of screens and user flows, subscription and payment logic, and whether a brand identity is included. After the discovery call, we provide an estimate based on the confirmed scope.

3

How long does it take to design a wellness app?

An MVP app design usually takes 3–4 weeks. A brand identity takes 1–3 weeks, and a UX audit of a live app takes 1–2 weeks.

Larger products are delivered in stages, so development can start before the whole design is finished.

4

How do you improve retention in a wellness app?

Cosmos Studio designs habit loops: a quick first win in onboarding, clear progress tracking, helpful reminders, and social accountability.

In Fastmates, users set personal goals in a few steps and find fasting mates nearby, so the habit is shared instead of tracked alone.

5

Can you design a subscription website for a wellness brand?

Yes. For Zeuss, Cosmos Studio built landing pages for five product lines, a GLP-1 weight loss quiz, and a personal account where customers manage subscriptions, orders, and payments.

The site runs on WooCommerce subscriptions and is optimized for mobile, where most customers shop.

6

Can you create a wellness brand that doesn’t look generic?

Yes. For Remroom, Cosmos Studio created a minimalist, typography-led identity with smooth shapes and a restrained palette, without typical wellness clichés.

7

Do you also develop the product?

Yes. Cosmos Studio develops websites and web apps in React, Next.js, and TypeScript. The same designers who design the product build its front end, design system, and motion. We call this UI engineering.

Native and cross-platform mobile apps are built by our vetted senior engineering partners under the same contract, with one point of contact.

8

Which agency can design a fitness or fasting app with social features?

Cosmos Studio designed Fastmates, an intermittent fasting app for iOS and Android. It combines a fasting tracker with social features, a radar to find nearby mates, and playful status characters that show mood at a glance.

9

Can you work under an NDA?
