    Pricing

    How much does it cost to work with LulaZel Hub?

    Projects start at R90,000, with hourly rates from R900. Specialized senior roles, such as AWS cloud architects, cost up to R3,060 per hour. The calculator above gives you a range in about 2 minutes. After a discovery call, you get a free, non-binding estimate based on the confirmed scope.

    Prices updated October 2026.

    Ways to work with us

    Three ways to start a project

    From R90,000

    A project with a fixed scope

    For a brand identity, a website, an MVP design, or a product redesign. We agree on scope, deliverables, and price before any work starts. Larger products are delivered in stages, so your developers can start before the whole design is finished.

    From R900 per hour

    A dedicated design team

    Senior designers and front-end engineers join your sprints as an embedded team, or take over the interface layer of your product. You pay for the hours the team works.

    From R36,000 for an AI-built MVP audit

    An audit first

    Not sure what to fix first? Start with a UX audit of your live product in 1–2 weeks. Built your MVP with Lovable, Bolt, or Cursor? Start with a code and UX audit before we finish and scale it.

    Typical timelines

    How long a project takes

    A typical LulaZel Hub project takes 2–6 weeks. You see the first results within a week of kickoff, and a clickable coded prototype takes from 1 week. These timelines assume you send feedback and materials on time.

    01

    Landing page

    1–2 weeks, from research to a responsive build.

    02

    Brand identity

    1–3 weeks, from strategy and concepts to the brand guidelines.

    03

    Multi-page website with CMS

    2–4 weeks, depending on pages, animation, and integrations.

    04

    MVP app design

    3–4 weeks, including research, flows, UI, and an interactive prototype. With front-end development: 4–8 weeks.

    05

    UX audit

    1–2 weeks, from your metrics and real usage data to a ranked list of fixes.

    06

    Product redesign

    Delivered in stages, starting with the flows that lose users first, so the product stays live.

    What affects the price

    Six things that move your estimate

    01

    User roles and flows

    An admin panel, an operator console, and a client portal are three products. Every extra role adds flows to research and design.

    02

    Platforms

    Web, iOS, Android, or desktop. Each platform follows its own guidelines and needs its own layouts.

    03

    Design system size

    A few reusable components for a landing page, or a full system in Figma and React code for a growing product.

    04

    Motion, 3D, and content

    -level animation, 3D product visuals AI-generated video take more time than static layouts.

    05

    Front-end development

    Design only, or design plus a production front end in React, Next.js, or Webflow with your integrations.

    06

    Research depth

    If you already know your customers, we start from hypotheses. If not, we run customer interviews with The Mom Test method first.

    Compare your options

    Freelancer, in-house hire, or a senior studio?

    Lowest price, most coordination

    A freelancer

    Usually the lowest rate. One person covers what they know best, so research, UI, motion, and front end often need several freelancers you coordinate yourself.

    Long-term cost, slow start

    An in-house senior designer

    Full-time salary plus benefits, and months to hire and onboard. One person rarely covers research, UI, the design system, and front-end code.

    From R90,000 or R900 per hour

    LulaZel Hub

    A senior team with research, UX, UI, motion, and front-end engineers under one contract. You can scale the team up or down by stage.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    1

    What is the minimum budget for a project?

    Projects start at R90,000, with hourly rates from R900.

    An audit of an MVP built with Lovable, Bolt, or Cursor starts from R36,000 and takes 1–2 weeks.

    2

    Why don’t you publish a fixed price list?

    Two products with the same number of screens can differ by several times in user roles, flows, and platforms.

    The calculator gives you a range in about 2 minutes, and the estimate after the discovery call is free and non-binding.

    3

    How do you estimate a project?

    After a discovery call about your product, customers, and goals, we confirm the scope and deliverables.

    Then LulaZel Hub sends a free, non-binding estimate with the price and timeline. No work starts before you agree.

    4

    What are your hourly rates?

    Hourly rates start at R900.

    Specialized senior roles, such as AWS cloud architects, can cost up to R3,060 per hour.

    5

    How long does a project take?

    A typical project takes 2–6 weeks. A landing page takes 1–2 weeks, a brand identity 1–3 weeks, a multi-page CMS website 2–4 weeks, MVP app design 3–4 weeks (4–8 weeks with front-end development), and a UX audit 1–2 weeks.

    6

    Can you work under an NDA?

    Yes. Every project can operate under an NDA from the first call.

    7

    Do you also develop what you design?

    Yes. We build production front ends in React, Next.js, and TypeScript, and websites on Webflow, WordPress, or custom code.

    Backend, mobile apps, and cloud are delivered by our vetted senior engineering partners under the same contract.
