FAQ

Frequently asked questions

1

What does a UI/UX design studio do?

A UI/UX design studio designs how a digital product works and looks: research, user flows, interface design, and a design system.

LulaZel Hub also builds the front end in React and Next.js, so the same team takes your product from the first customer interview to working code.

2

How much does UI/UX design cost?

Projects start at R90,000, with hourly rates from R900. Specialized senior roles, such as AWS cloud architects, can cost up to R3,060 per hour.

After the discovery call, you get a free, non-binding estimate based on the confirmed scope. For a quick range, try our project cost calculator.

3

How long does a project take?

A typical project takes 2–6 weeks. A landing page takes 1–2 weeks, a brand identity 1–3 weeks, a multi-page CMS website 2–4 weeks, MVP app design 3–4 weeks (4–8 weeks with front-end development), and a UX audit 1–2 weeks.

Larger products are delivered in stages, so your developers can start before the whole design is finished.

4

How do we start working together?

Book a discovery call. We learn about your product, customers, and goals, and you can ask for an NDA from the first call.

Then we agree on scope, deliverables, and price before any work starts.

5

Why do you start with research?

Because design built on guesses is expensive to fix later. If you already know your customers’ needs, we go straight to product hypotheses.

If not, we run customer interviews using The Mom Test method, or give you the questions to ask your customers.

6

What does working with you look like week to week?

You see the first results within a week of kickoff. Designs and staging builds are reviewed every week, and nothing moves to the next stage without your sign-off.

Day-to-day questions go through Slack or WhatsApp. The team works 10:00–18:00 Kyiv time, which overlaps with US East Coast mornings.

7

Who will work on my project?

A dedicated senior team: a product or UI/UX designer and a project manager as your single point of contact. Our design director Alex Chaika reviews every deliverable, and founder Lulamile Mkhungela joins reviews of key stages.

8

Do you also develop the product?

Yes. We build production front ends in React, Next.js, and TypeScript, and websites on Webflow, WordPress, or custom code.

Backend, mobile apps, and cloud are delivered by our vetted senior engineering partners under the same contract.

9

Can you work with our in-house team?

Yes. Our designers can join your sprints as an embedded design team, or hand off a design system and code to your developers.

10

Which industries do you work with?

Defense and military tech, SaaS, AI products, EdTech, MedTech and healthcare, Web3, gaming, B2B and IT services, hospitality, health and wellness, and nonprofits.

11

Can you work under an NDA?

Yes. Every project can operate under an NDA from the first call.

12

Can we talk to your past clients?

Yes. We share client contacts for references on request. You can also read 21 verified reviews on our Clutch profile and contact the reviewers there.
