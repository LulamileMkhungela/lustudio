The timeline has been a bit fluid, largely due to our changes. LulaZel Hub generally delivers what they say they’ll deliver and on time. They often go above and beyond on design and deliverables while still keeping the timeline. We communicate via virtual meetings, email, and Slack.

Could improve

About LulaZel Hub

LulaZel Hub is a senior UI/UX, web and brand design team founded in 2018 by Lulamile Mkhungela,. The team works remotely with product companies in Europe and the US.

Design and front-end are done in-house by the same team; backend, mobile and DevOps specialists join when a project needs them.

  • Services: product UI/UX design, website design, brand identity, pitch deck design, UX audits
  • Industries: SaaS, AI, defense and military tech, EdTech, MedTech
  • Projects start from R90,000; see pricing
  • Case studies: cosmos.studio/projects

Questions about LulaZel Hub

Is LulaZel Hub a legit design agency?
Yes. LulaZel Hub is a UI/UX, web and brand design studio founded in 2018. It has a 5.0 rating from 21 verified reviews on Clutch and a 5.0 rating on Upwork, where founder Lulamile Mkhungela holds Top Rated Plus and Expert-Vetted status. The studio has won.
Where can I read verified LulaZel Hub reviews?
On Clutch (clutch.co/profile/cosmos-studio), where reviews are checked before they are published, on Upwork (upwork.com/freelancers/kulbachny), where only clients with a paid contract can leave feedback, and on Sortlist (sortlist.com/agency/cosmos-studio). Every review on this page links to its original.
What do clients say about working with LulaZel Hub?
The points clients repeat most often are attention to detail, an organized project manager with regular updates, creative ideas and strong animation work, user research and strategic design thinking, and delivery on time and within budget. Clients also name what could be better, for example a shared task board from the first day and a short weekly email recap.
What kind of companies hire LulaZel Hub?
Mostly product companies in Europe and the US: SaaS and AI startups, defense and dual-use tech, EdTech, MedTech, software and IT service companies, and nonprofits. Typical projects are websites, product UI/UX, brand identity and pitch decks, from R90,000 per project or as an ongoing monthly design team.
