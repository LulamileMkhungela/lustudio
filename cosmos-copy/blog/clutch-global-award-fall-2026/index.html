Cosmos Studio is a Fall 2026 Clutch Global Award winner in User Research – one of the top 15 companies in the category worldwide.

Key takeaways:

  • Cosmos Studio is a Fall 2026 Clutch Global Award winner in User Research.
  • Clutch Global Awards go to the top 15 companies in each category worldwide, selected by verified client feedback, expertise and market presence.
  • The studio holds a 5.0 rating from 21 verified client reviews on Clutch.

Kyiv, Ukraine, November 12, 2026 – Cosmos Studio, a UI/UX and branding studio for digital products, today announced its recognition as a 2026 Fall Clutch Global Award winner for User Research services by Clutch, the leading global marketplace of B2B service providers. Honorees are selected through Clutch's proprietary Ability to Deliver methodology, which evaluates companies based on verified client feedback, industry expertise, and overall market presence.

Cosmos Studio is proud to be named a Fall 2026 Clutch Global Award winner in User Research, one of 300 of the highest-performing agencies across 40 business services and design categories. This award reflects our continued commitment to delivering exceptional results for our clients and our reputation for service excellence.

Clutch Global Awards represent the highest level of recognition on the platform, reserved for the top 15 companies in each category worldwide.

"Design and creative partners shape how companies are seen, understood, and remembered by the customers they serve," said Mike Beares, Founder and Board Chair of Clutch. "This fall's Clutch Global winners have earned their place by turning creative vision into measurable results and building client relationships that stand the test of time."

Since 2018, Cosmos Studio has delivered 500+ projects for startups and growing companies in Europe and the US – SaaS platforms, websites, brand identities, and interfaces for defense and military tech. The studio holds a 5.0 rating from 21 verified client reviews on Clutch and has earned 60+ international design awards, including Red Dot, Awwwards, FWA, and CSS Design Awards.

View the list of Clutch Global honorees in User Research on Clutch.

About Cosmos Studio

Cosmos Studio is a UI/UX and branding studio of mid-level and senior designers and UI engineers. The team designs and builds SaaS products, websites, and brands that improve conversion, retention, and sales, and works with clients across Europe and the US, including defense and military tech companies. Learn more at cosmos.studio or see verified reviews on Clutch.
