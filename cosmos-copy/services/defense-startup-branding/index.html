About LulaZel Hub

Look credible before you can show the product

Defense startups often need to win investors, partners and first contracts before the product can be shown in public. The brand, website and deck carry that first impression. LulaZel Hub builds them as one system that feels precise, engineering-led and trustworthy.

We created the brand and websites for Bravo1Alpha, the visual style, 3D renders and website for SerenX, and the Sarmat Analytix brand with Obriy Design Büro. SerenX asked us to avoid the dark, generic look common among defense startups, and its website was shared with around 100 prospective government customers and partners before launch.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

1

What is included in defense startup branding?

Logo and visual identity, brand guidelines, website, pitch deck and presentation templates, and 3D product visuals. You can start with the identity and add the rest as you grow.

2

What defense brands have you created?

We created the brand and websites for Bravo1Alpha, a European GEOINT provider, the corporate visual style and website for SerenX, a US interceptor maker, and the Sarmat Analytix brand with Obriy Design Büro.

3

How much does it cost and how long does it take?

Projects start at R90,000, with hourly rates from R900. A brand identity usually takes 1–3 weeks. A multi-page website takes 2–4 weeks, and a landing page 1–2 weeks.

4

Can you brand a product that is not public yet?

Yes. We work under NDA, and websites for unannounced products stay in private preview environments until you approve the launch.

5

Do you create 3D renders of our hardware?

Yes. We created 3D hardware renders for SerenX and can visualize your product from CAD files or references, even before it is ready to photograph.

6

Do you design pitch decks for investors and government buyers?

Yes. We design investor decks and government presentations in the same visual system as your brand and website, so every touchpoint looks consistent.
